CHINA WARNS US OVER AI RULES

BY BEIJING CORRESPONDENT • JUST NOW

China has strongly criticized new US actions related to artificial intelligence. It declared that the United States is attempting to control and dominate critical AI technologies under the guise of security regulations.

“AI technology should benefit all humanity, not be controlled by any single nation.”

Beijing warned that it may take necessary countermeasures to protect its domestic technological development and ensure fair access to international AI advancements.
